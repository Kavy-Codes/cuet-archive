04 How the archive stays alive

🔄 The pipeline

  1. Shared in Discord — members drop books, mocks and links in the resources forum.
  2. Synced automatically — the archive bot mirrors new posts on every sync.
  3. Published here — this site rebuilds with fresh pages, search and mocks.

❓ Quick answers

Are the mocks really free?
Yes. All 66 papers are hosted here and run entirely in your browser. No account, no paywall, no expiring download links.
Some file links ask me to join Discord?
Original Discord attachment links expire. Every mock is hosted on this site; for other files use the “Open in Discord” button on its card.
How do I request a missing resource?
Drop it in the request-resources forum — it lands in the archive on the next sync.
